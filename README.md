## About the course

Learners use Microsoft Foundry and Azure AI services to design secure AI solutions, build grounded generative apps and agents, work with image, text, and document content, and evaluate quality, safety, latency, and cost. The materials retain the approved WSQ learning outcomes and assessment criteria while reflecting the [current AI-103 skills guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ai-103).

## Learning outcomes

- Analyse Azure AI application algorithms and their relationship to efficiency.
- Evaluate the strengths and limitations of Azure AI applications.
- Assess feasibility and improvements in product and maintenance processes.
- Support decisions with architecture, runtime, metric, safety, and acceptance evidence.

## Topics covered

- Foundry projects, model selection, identity, networking, monitoring, and responsible AI.
- Generative AI, RAG, search, tools, agent orchestration, and evaluation.
- Computer vision, image generation, multimodal understanding, and video.
- Text analysis, speech, translation, document intelligence, and content understanding.
